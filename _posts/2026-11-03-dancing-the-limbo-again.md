I started writing this at [Devoxx Belgium](https://devoxx.be/) in Antwerp, and held it until the conference was over.
The conference was great, and I met so many people, even though starting those conversations felt strange and hard.
The overall spirit made up for it.

<!--more-->

My talk went well.
I covered everything I wanted to say, but I was rushing, out of breath, and more nervous than usual.
Then the audience feedback came in and confirmed it: too fast, and it looked like I was nervous.
That part is true, and reading it from someone else still hit differently than feeling it on stage.

What I didn't expect was what it set off afterwards.
One piece of feedback about pacing, and suddenly I was asking bigger questions.
Is this really what I'm good at?
Should I continue with it?
And underneath that, the question I've been circling since I went independent: is self-employment the right way forward, or should I go back into employment?

I know this feeling.
It's the same back-and-forth I wrote about in [Dancing the Limbo]({% post_url 2026-06-14-Dancing-the-limbo %}), just wearing different clothes.

Part of me wants to rush through the uncertainty, accept the path I chose, and stop re-evaluating commitments I made only weeks before.
Another part says I'm at the very beginning, with a lot to learn and a lot to look at, and that maybe the right move is to step back, explore other business relationships, and see what actually fits before treating anything as final.

I don't have an answer, and I'm not writing this to reach one.
Talking it through and writing it down is how I work on it, and the feeling loosens when I get it out of my head and look at it from the outside.

That's where I am for now.
